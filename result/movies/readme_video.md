# Supplementary Information Guide

*Quantum synchronization and chimera states in a programmable quantum many-body system*

This guide describes the four supplementary video files associated with Fig. 1 of the main text and Supplementary Fig. S11.

## Supplementary Video 1

**File:** `Supplementary_Video_1_MPS_weak_randomness.mp4`

**Title:** Time-resolved emergence of global synchronization in an MPS simulation of a 156-qubit heavy-hex lattice.

**Legend:** Each frame shows the local in-plane magnetization at all sites at integer stroboscopic times from $t/T = 0$ to 30 for weak initial phase randomness, $\varphi^{\max}/\pi = 1$. Starting from a disordered configuration, the magnetization progressively aligns and forms a globally synchronized state. Results are obtained from MPS simulations with bond dimension $\chi = 600$ on the $L = 156$ heavy-hex lattice. Parameters are $(\theta_{XX}, \theta_{ZZ}, \theta_Z) = (-0.25\pi, -0.25\pi, 0.25\pi)$. Arrow lengths are rescaled independently in each frame for clarity.

## Supplementary Video 2

**File:** `Supplementary_Video_2_MPS_strong_randomness.mp4`

**Title:** Time-resolved emergence of a quantum chimera state in an MPS simulation of a 156-qubit heavy-hex lattice.

**Legend:** Each frame shows the local in-plane magnetization at all sites at integer stroboscopic times from $t/T = 0$ to 30 for strong initial phase randomness, $\varphi^{\max}/\pi = 2$. Starting from a disordered configuration, the magnetization develops spatially separated regions that retain internal phase coherence while remaining mutually incoherent, characteristic of a quantum chimera state. Results are obtained from MPS simulations with bond dimension $\chi = 600$ on the $L = 156$ heavy-hex lattice. Parameters are $(\theta_{XX}, \theta_{ZZ}, \theta_Z) = (-0.25\pi, -0.25\pi, 0.25\pi)$. Arrow lengths are rescaled independently in each frame for clarity.

## Supplementary Video 3

**File:** `Supplementary_Video_3_qdevice_weak_randomness.mp4`

**Title:** Time-resolved experimental emergence of global synchronization on a 156-qubit heavy-hex quantum processor.

**Legend:** Each frame shows the reconstructed in-plane magnetization on the $L = 156$ heavy-hex lattice at integer stroboscopic times from $t/T = 0$ to 30 for weak initial phase randomness, $\varphi^{\max}/\pi = 1$. At $t/T = 0$, the magnetization is determined by the randomly selected preparation phases. At later times, it is reconstructed from the measured transverse magnetization $\langle \hat{X}_j(t) \rangle$ using the analytic-signal representation. The dynamics develop global phase alignment. Data are obtained on ibm_kobe with parameters $(\theta_{XX}, \theta_{ZZ}, \theta_Z) = (-0.25\pi, -0.25\pi, 0.25\pi)$. Arrow lengths are rescaled independently in each frame for clarity.
